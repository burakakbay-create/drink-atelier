Drink Atelier — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 June 2026

Drink Atelier (“the App”) is a casual puzzle game. This policy explains what data the App and its third-party services handle.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your game progress (levels, coins, settings) is stored only on your device and is not sent to us.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect limited data as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising): to show ads, it may access your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA), device information, general app-usage data and approximate location. You can remove all ads with the one-time “Remove Ads” in-app purchase. See Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data.
  • Google Firebase Analytics: collects anonymous app-usage events, device/app info and approximate location (derived from IP) to help us understand how the game is played.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics: collects crash reports and device state to help us fix bugs and improve stability.

App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, the App asks for your permission before using the advertising identifier for personalized ads. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized. You can change this any time in iOS Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and ads are not behaviorally targeted to children.

Your Choices

  • Buy “Remove Ads” to turn off all advertising.
  • Decline App Tracking Transparency for non-personalized ads.
  • Reset or limit your advertising identifier in iOS Settings.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above reflects the most recent revision.
