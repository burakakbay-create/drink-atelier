Drink Atelier — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 June 2026

Drink Atelier (“the App”) is a single-player, offline puzzle game. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how the App handles data.

Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. The App does not require an account and does not request your name, email, location, contacts, or any identifiers. The App does not track you across other apps or websites.

On-Device Data

Your game progress (levels completed, coins, and settings) is stored only on your device. It never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or to any third party. Removing the App deletes this data.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. Apple’s handling of purchase data is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Third Parties

The App does not include advertising or analytics SDKs that collect personal data. If this changes in a future version, this policy and the App’s App Store privacy details will be updated accordingly.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above reflects the most recent revision.
